# ADR-0000: Template

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Affects:** subsystem(s)
- **Supersedes:** — · **Superseded by:** —

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraints, the pressure, what was already true. Enough
that a reader in two years understands the situation without asking anyone.

Keep it factual. Save the argument for Alternatives.

## Decision

What is now true, stated in one or two sentences, in the present tense.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| The obvious alternative | The specific reason it loses |
| The thing someone will propose in six months | The specific reason it loses |
| Do nothing | What that costs |

This is the most valuable section. An ADR without real alternatives is a changelog
entry.

## Consequences

What follows — good and bad. Include the costs; an ADR that only lists benefits is
not being honest about a trade.

- Positive consequence
- Cost or constraint accepted
- What this forecloses

## Reopen when

_(Optional, for deferrals.)_ The conditions under which this should be revisited.
Written as a test, not a feeling: "when X exists and Y is measured", not "when we
have time".
